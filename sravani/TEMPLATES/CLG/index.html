Why Green Valley Junior College?
Founded with a passion for educational excellence, Green Valley Junior College has been serving students and surrounding communities with distinction. It is the mission of the college to empower students to discover opportunities and reach their career goals through open access and supportive higher education. As a trusted institution, Green Valley Junior College creates and promotes equity in education for every member of our diverse population. We are your community's college.
